Seasonality

Best Time To Visit Different Regions of India

Winter (Oct - Mar): Perfect for Rajasthan, Goa beaches, South India temple circuits, and Golden Triangle.
Summer (Apr - Jun): Ideal for escaping the heat in Manali, Kashmir, Shimla, Ooty, and Darjeeling.
Monsoon (Jul - Sep): Spectacular lush green landscapes in Kerala backwaters, Western Ghats, and waterfalls.

Fleet Advice

How To Select The Right Vehicle For Your Group

12 / 17 Seater Tempo Traveller: Best for extended families (8-15 persons), wedding guests, or corporate teams.
Volvo Multi-Axle Bus (45-50 seats): Ideal for school/college tours, large pilgrim groups, and company outings.
AC Mini Coach (25-30 seats): Perfect for mid-sized family events, corporate outings, and comfortable group travel.

Pilgrimage Tips

South India Temple Circuit Guidelines

Dress Code: Traditional attire (Dhoti/Kurta for men, Sarees/Salwar for women) is mandatory at Tirumala Tirupati, Madurai, and Srirangam.
Darshan Booking: We assist in advance Seeghra Darshan slots and schedule coach departures to match timing.
Senior Citizens: Our buses feature low-entry boarding and rest stops every 2-3 hours.

Telangana & Andhra Pradesh Travel Info

As Hyderabad's premier transport provider since 2000, we specialize in twin cities and Telugu state circuits.

Telangana (TS Info)

Telangana offers a rich tapestry of Nizami heritage, UNESCO world heritage sites, and modern entertainment.

  • Hyderabad Twin Cities: Golconda Fort, Charminar, Salar Jung Museum, Hussain Sagar, Birla Mandir.
  • Ramoji Film City: World's largest integrated film city with full-day VBST coach tours.
  • Warangal Circuit: Ramappa Temple (UNESCO), Thousand Pillar Temple, and Laknavaram Lake.

Andhra Pradesh (AP Info)

Andhra Pradesh is renowned for sacred pilgrim destinations, scenic beaches in Vizag, and the lush Papikondalu hills.

  • Tirupati & Tirumala: Lord Venkateswara Temple, Srikalahasti Rahu-Ketu Kshetram, Kanipakam.
  • Srisailam Jyotirlinga: Mallikarjuna Swamy Temple nestled in dense Nallamala forest.
  • Visakhapatnam & Araku: Rushikonda Beach, Borra Caves, and coffee plantations.
